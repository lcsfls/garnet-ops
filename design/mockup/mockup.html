    Settings

    Local AI

    Any endpoint that speaks the OpenAI API.

    vLLM, Ollama, LiteLLM, LM Studio. Must be inside your network, otherwise you get a warning.
    Stored encrypted and never shown again.
    Secrets are always removed before sending.

    Good to know

    Why local only?

    Findings contain excerpts of your code. A cloud endpoint would send them outside your network.

    Which model?

    Code models from about 14 B parameters triage findings reliably. Smaller models explain well but propose weaker fixes.

    No GPU?

    Scans and gates work without AI. Only the triage is missing.
